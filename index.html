FAQ

Common questions.

Don't see yours here? Just send a quick message — I'll get back to you the same day.

How does the monthly plan work? +
You pay a small one-time setup fee, then a flat monthly price. That covers your website, hosting, ongoing maintenance, and a set amount of monthly updates.
How long does it take to build my website? +
Most landing pages are ready in about 5–7 days. Three to five page business sites take 10–14 days. Premium sites usually run 2–3 weeks. I'll give you a clear timeline before we start.
What counts as a monthly update? +
Things like updating your hours, adding new photos, changing prices, swapping out testimonials, fixing typos, or tweaking text. Each plan includes a set amount of update time per month — 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 45 minutes, 1 hour, or 2 hours depending on the plan.
Do I need to provide my own photos and logo? +
Photos and a logo help, but they're not required to start. I can use professional stock photos that fit your business, and I can point you to options if you don't have a logo yet.
Will the website work on phones? +
Yes. Every site is built mobile-first, because most of your customers will see it on their phone. It will look clean and load properly on phones, tablets, and computers.
Can customers contact me through the site? +
Yes. Every site comes with a contact form that delivers messages straight to your email, plus click-to-call buttons so customers can ring you with one tap on their phone.
Is this a marketing service? Do you handle SEO or ads? +
My focus is websites, not full-service marketing. I don't do advanced SEO, paid ads, or blog writing. Google Business Profile setup is available — it's included free on Small Business Pro and up, or available as an $79 one-time add-on for any plan. I build you a clean, professional website and keep it running. That's it.
Do you guarantee leads or sales? +
No. A professional website helps your business look more trustworthy and gives customers an easy way to reach you — but I won't promise specific leads or sales. Anyone who guarantees that is misleading you.
